90/180 Privacy Policy

App: 90/180 Schengen Calculator (com.days90180.filipiumlabs) · Developer: Filipium Labs · Effective 14 October 2026

The short version

90/180 does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

What the app stores

  • The days you mark as abroad, your day limit, and your theme, colour, language and warning settings.
  • This data is saved only in the app's private storage on your device.

Internet and third parties

The app does not request internet access, so it cannot send data anywhere. It contains no ads, analytics, crash reporting or tracking SDKs and uses no advertising ID.

Backups

  • Export data writes a backup file to a location you choose. The app never sends it anywhere; what happens to it afterwards is up to you.
  • If you have turned on Android's own device backup, Android may include the app's settings in your Google account backup. That is controlled by your phone's settings, not by the app.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app shows local warnings when you get close to your limit. They are generated on the device; no notification service or server is involved.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Clear all days, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: gorancubra@gmail.com.

90/180 Politika privatnosti

Aplikacija: 90/180 Šengen kalkulator (com.days90180.filipiumlabs) · Razvojni tim: Filipium Labs · Važi od 14. oktobra 2026.

Ukratko

90/180 ne prikuplja lične podatke, ne čuva ih na serveru, ne deli ih i ne prodaje. Sve što unesete ostaje na vašem telefonu.

Šta aplikacija čuva

  • Dane koje označite kao dane u inostranstvu, vaš limit dana i podešavanja teme, boje, jezika i upozorenja.
  • Ovi podaci se čuvaju samo u privatnom prostoru aplikacije na vašem uređaju.

Internet i treće strane

Aplikacija ne traži pristup internetu, pa ne može nigde da šalje podatke. Nema reklame, analitiku, prijavu grešaka ni praćenje i ne koristi reklamni ID.

Rezervne kopije

  • Izvezi podatke pravi datoteku na mestu koje vi izaberete. Aplikacija je nikome ne šalje; dalje njome raspolažete vi.
  • Ako ste uključili Android rezervnu kopiju uređaja, Android može da uključi podešavanja aplikacije u rezervnu kopiju na vašem Google nalogu. To kontrolišu podešavanja telefona, a ne aplikacija.

Obaveštenja

Ako dozvolite obaveštenja, aplikacija prikazuje lokalna upozorenja kada se približite limitu. Nastaju na uređaju, bez ikakvog servera.

Brisanje podataka

Koristite Podešavanja → Obriši sve dane, obrišite podatke aplikacije u podešavanjima Androida ili deinstalirajte aplikaciju.

Izmene i kontakt

Ako se ova politika promeni, nova verzija biće objavljena na ovoj stranici sa novim datumom. Pitanja: gorancubra@gmail.com.
